About the Company

M/S Ripa Enterprise

A longstanding Bangladesh-based contractor serving civil construction, infrastructure and government project requirements.

Government Civil Construction & Contracting

M/S Ripa Enterprise is a proprietorship construction and contracting business established in 1999. The company has been involved in government projects since 2001.

Its project experience covers civil and structural construction, water supply and pipeline works, sewerage and drainage, canal and waterway works, earthwork, roads, buildings, maintenance and rehabilitation.

More than 260 projects have been completed over approximately 25 years of operation.

Public-Sector Experience

The company's project experience includes work associated with public-sector organizations and government engineering activities.

Relevant organizations include DWASA, BWDB, BIWTA, BAPEX/Petrobangla, LGED, EED and Bangladesh Army-related engineering activities.

The project register presented on this website contains representative project descriptions and does not represent the complete 260+ project portfolio.

Leadership & Heritage

From a Foundation of Service to Modern Leadership

Ripa Enterprise combines its founding heritage with modern management and forward-looking business development.

Founder & Heritage

Md. Abdul Jalil

Md. Abdul Jalil founded M/S Ripa Enterprise. A heroic Freedom Fighter, he participated in the 1971 Liberation War.

He joined the Bangladesh Army in 1974 and served as a soldier. His experience and values helped shape the company's culture of discipline, responsibility, integrity, teamwork, commitment and duty.

Proprietor & Second-Generation Leadership

Mohammed Saiem Islam

Mohammed Saiem Islam is the Proprietor of M/S Ripa Enterprise and provides strategic direction for management, operations, project development and business growth.

He brings more than 18 years of professional experience across technology, business development, project management, administration, customer service, engineering, construction, government projects, digital marketing and business operations.

He also serves as Co-Founder & CEO of Laaref Technology, a technology company based in New York, USA.
