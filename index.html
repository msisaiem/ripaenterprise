COMPANY OVERVIEW

Field-oriented capability with public-sector experience.

Ripa Enterprise has developed practical experience working on projects associated with Dhaka Water Supply and Sewerage Authority (DWASA), Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB), Bangladesh Inland Water Transport Authority (BIWTA), Bangladesh Petroleum Exploration & Production Company Limited (BAPEX), Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), Education Engineering Department (EED), and projects associated with Bangladesh Army engineering activities.

The documented portfolio covers water-supply pipelines, sewerage and drainage, canal and river works, bank protection, earthwork, road restoration, boundary walls, pump-house and compound works, institutional buildings, rehabilitation and maintenance.

FOUNDER, HERITAGE & LEADERSHIP

Built on discipline, responsibility and duty.

Founder & Heritage

Heroic Freedom Fighter Md. Abdul Jalil founded M/S Ripa Enterprise and established its early foundation. During the 1971 Liberation War, he participated in the struggle for Bangladesh’s independence. Following the Liberation War, he joined the Bangladesh Army in 1974 and served as a soldier.

His experience shaped a working culture built around discipline, responsibility, integrity, teamwork, commitment and duty. These principles became part of the enterprise’s identity as it developed practical experience in construction and public-sector works.

Second-Generation Leadership

The enterprise has now transitioned into its second generation of leadership. Mohammed Saiem Islam serves as the Proprietor of M/S Ripa Enterprise, providing strategic direction and overseeing management, operations, project development, business growth and the company’s future vision.

Building upon the strong foundation established by his father, Heroic Freedom Fighter Md. Abdul Jalil, Mohammed Saiem Islam brings a modern management perspective to Ripa Enterprise while preserving the core values of integrity, discipline, responsibility and commitment that have guided the enterprise since its early years.

Mohammed Saiem Islam is a technology entrepreneur, business leader and multidisciplinary professional with more than 18 years of professional experience spanning technology, business development, project management, administration, customer service, engineering, construction, government projects, digital marketing and business operations.

In addition to leading Ripa Enterprise, Mohammed Saiem Islam is Co-Founder & CEO of Laaref Technology, a New York, USA-based technology company focused on innovative digital solutions. Its flagship product, Drivelaar, is an all-in-one driver platform designed for NYC drivers, bringing ticket management, defensive driving, insurance, DMV/TLC requirements and essential driver alerts together in one platform.
